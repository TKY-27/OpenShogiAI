# Dataset License Pending

Dataset licensing remains source-scoped rather than inherited from the source-code license.

The exact 100-object AobaZero no-noise sample catalog audited in Phase 3 is approved as Public
Domain for machine-learning use and redistribution; see `docs/source-audits/aobazero.md`.
That decision does not cover other AobaZero files, bulk archives, weights, Floodgate, or future
sources. Local Phase 3 artifacts are not committed or externally published.

The AGPL-3.0-only source-code license does not apply automatically to future datasets. A dataset
license may be assigned only after every included source has complete provenance, explicit
machine-learning permission, compatible redistribution terms, and a reviewed manifest.

Until each additional source is separately approved, it must not enter training or be
published or represented as redistributable.
